  • Jaeger-LeCoultre

Sleek, sophisticated and often times a little surprising, it is impossible not to love the timepiece creations from Manufacture Jaeger-LeCoultre. Nestled snugly in the heart of the Vallée de Joux, in the Swiss Jura Mountains, the company has been making substantial contributions to the world of horology for the better part of 190 years. This includes the development and production of over 1,200 different calibres, the registration of approximately 400 patents and the creation of hundreds of inventions and designs. Some of which are now considered the most iconic and astonishing models in history, such as the breath-takingly complex Master Grande Tradition Gyrotourbillon Westminster Perpétuel.

PURCHASE

Equally impressive is the fact that, as much as possible, Jaeger-LeCoultre does everything in-house. And we do mean everything. One hundred and eighty different expert skills are brought together under one roof, to breathe life into the heart of the watches and to animate each infinitely small component. Design, assemble, decorate or crimp; every different stage required for the creation of a Jaeger-LeCoultre timepiece is conducted within the Manufacture.

It’s no surprise then that the company has a rich history of loyal clients, many of whom are repeat customers. Current friends of the brand include Lenny Kravitz, Nicholas Hoult and Anya-Taylor Joy. Individuals who value quality and craftmanship, but also inventiveness and innovation.

Today, Jaeger-LeCoultre expresses its values of invention, tradition, design, aesthetics and joaillerie through each of its eight collections: Reverso, Rendez-Vous, Master, Polaris, Geophysic, Duomètre, Atmos and Hybris Mechanica.

Jaeger-LeCoultre is available at our Watches of Switzerland Boutiques in Adelaide, Melbourne City, Sydney and Perth. For enquiries contact 1800 808 135 or click the button below. As an authorised Jaeger-LeCoultre Service Centre, we provide an after sales service for the lifetime of your Jaeger-LeCoultre watch.
