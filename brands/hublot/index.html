The Art of Fusion

The “Art of Fusion”. This is the creative concept that underpins everything that Swiss luxury watch brand Hublot does. It’s what led company founder, Carlo Crocco, to put a rubber strap on the gold case of his first Hublot watch in 1980. A concept that, at the time, was completely unheard of and considered absolutely outrageous. This wasn’t just any old rubber strap though. Three years of research and development had gone into creating the luxury watch industry’s first natural rubber strap. And it didn’t take too long for watch lovers to realise all that effort had paid off. Today, the trend is widespread and Hublot continues to innovate with new materials.

In more recent times the brand has become known for its mastery of more complex materials, such as carbon fibre and sapphire. The latter is a notoriously difficult material to work with. But one that, in the right hands, produces absolutely stunning results. A prime example being the recently released Spirit of Big Bang Tourbillon Sapphire. The brand has also pioneered the development of coloured ceramics, resulting in eye-catching models such as the Big Bang Integral Blue Ceramic. A model which features a case and integrated bracelet made entirely from satin finished and polished blue ceramic.

As a vertically-integrated manufacturer, Hublot has also proved its watchmaking prowess through the development and manufacture of a high-end movements in-house. These can be found in the iconic Big Bang, King Power, Classic Fusion and Masterpiece collections, which house watch complications such as the tourbillon, minute repeater, split second and retrograde date, to name a few.
