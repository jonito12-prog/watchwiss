Watches of Switzerland Presents

High-Watchmaking

Luxury watch enthusiasts, discerning collectors, and true connoisseurs of horology are invited to explore a remarkable celebration of fine timekeeping with Watches of Switzerland’s exclusive High Watchmaking collection. This distinguished showcase presents a meticulously curated selection of timepieces that represent the highest standards of craftsmanship, innovation, and tradition.

Breguet

A name synonymous with horological innovation, Breguet has shaped the very foundations of watchmaking. From inventing the tourbillon to perfecting guilloché dials and blued Breguet hands, the brand remains a beacon of classical sophistication and invention. Breguet’s high complications reflect not just historical achievement but an ongoing dedication to artistry and excellence, capturing the spirit of one of watchmaking’s most influential founders.

Hublot

Defying convention with every creation, Hublot brings a modern and avant-garde spirit to high watchmaking. Famed for its Art of Fusion philosophy, the maison merges traditional Swiss craftsmanship with groundbreaking materials such as carbon fibre, ceramic, and Magic Gold. From the powerful Big Bang Unico to the architectural MP series, Hublot’s timepieces showcase bold design, in-house movements, and a fearless approach to innovation — making each watch a contemporary statement of mechanical artistry.

Roger Dubuis

Roger Dubuis stands at the cutting edge of contemporary watchmaking, pushing the boundaries of design, performance, and complication. With its bold architecture, skeletonised calibres, and Poinçon de Genève certification, each timepiece is a dramatic expression of Hyper Horology™. From the Excalibur collection’s signature star-shaped bridges to the thrilling mechanics of its minute repeaters and tourbillons, Roger Dubuis creates watches for those who demand both technical excellence and radical individuality.

Vacheron Constantin

With over 265 years of uninterrupted watchmaking history, Vacheron Constantin is the embodiment of timeless elegance and technical mastery. The maison’s creations blend heritage with innovation, showcasing intricate complications, refined aesthetics, and exceptional finishing. From the sleek sophistication of the Patrimony to the architectural complexity of the Overseas and Traditionnelle collections, each timepiece is a testament to the brand’s enduring dedication to fine watchmaking. Vacheron Constantin doesn’t just measure time — it elevates it.

Cartier

With a legacy defined by creativity and innovation, Cartier High Watchmaking pushes the boundaries of horological artistry. The maison’s timepieces unite bold design with technical prowess, from skeletonised movements and mystery complications to sculptural cases adorned with precious gems. Each creation reflects Cartier’s ability to transform timekeeping into an art form, where craftsmanship, elegance, and ingenuity meet. Cartier does not just craft watches, it reimagines them.
